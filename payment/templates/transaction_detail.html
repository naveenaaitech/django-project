Trainer Concept Reference

1. Separation of Concerns: Payment Status reflects whether funds were authorized by the bank. Order Status reflects fulfillment & shipping state.

2. Smallest Currency Unit: Financial APIs require paise (integers) to prevent floating-point rounding errors during calculations.

3. Cryptographic Proof: The backend computes HMAC-SHA256(order_id + '|' + payment_id, secret_key) and compares it to signature before marking the order as paid.
